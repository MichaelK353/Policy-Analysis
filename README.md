# Policy-Analysis
Policy Analysis Research

1. **Industrial Strategy for Ireland:** Updated Industrial Policy Strategy for Ireland focussed on delivering innovation by expanding financing strategically.

2. **Transport Strategy for Viet Nam:** Encorporating anticipatory forecasting techniques to update Viet Nam's Transport and Air Quality policy.

3. **Populism and Path Dependence:** Research paper analysing whether Populism reduces path dependence in Public Policy.

1. **Technology Policy Stringency and Greenhouse Gas Emissions:** Statistical/Econometric analysis of the relationship between technology policy stringency and GHG Emissions reductions. First Difference OLS and Differenence-in-Difference models were used to investigate causality. Policy recommendations were made based on the findings.

2. **Driving Down Ireland's Transport Emissions:** This writing sample analyses empirical transport emissions data in Ireland, highlighting that they are still closely coupled with economic activity. The current policy measures used to reduce these emissions are discussed and evaluated, and further measures are proposed. The potential of rail infrastructure and Market-based cap-and-trade systems are discussed.
